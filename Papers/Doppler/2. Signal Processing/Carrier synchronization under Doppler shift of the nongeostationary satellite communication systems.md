### Persistent Notes
This paper studies Doppler in **non-geostationary satellite communications**, specifically the problem of recovering a Doppler-shifted carrier at a receiver. It does **not** attempt to use Doppler measurements to estimate a satellite's orbit.

The relevant connection is that it demonstrates something fundamental about satellite Doppler: as a satellite moves relative to a receiver, the changing separation causes a Doppler shift, and for LEO satellites both the **Doppler shift and its rate of change can be large**.

he Doppler shift changes sign around closest approach, and the **Doppler rate** also varies substantially. The authors therefore stress that for LEO you cannot regard Doppler as simply a constant frequency offset; its dynamics matter.
