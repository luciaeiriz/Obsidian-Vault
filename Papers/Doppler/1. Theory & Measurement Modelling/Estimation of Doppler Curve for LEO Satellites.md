### Persistent Notes
Relevant to understanding the physics and geometry behind satellite Doppler, but does not actually perform OD (inverse OD problem that what we are trying to do). 

They mention at the end that if Doppler observations were obtained from **three or more ground stations**, the orbital parameters could instead be recovered in the reverse process, which they call orbit determination, but they don't actually perform it. 

#### Relevance to RODINN
Inverse problem. Section **2.5 Doppler shift** connects Doppler to range-rate.
