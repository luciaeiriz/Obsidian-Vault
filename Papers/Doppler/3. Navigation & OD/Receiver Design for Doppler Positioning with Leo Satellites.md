### Persistent Notes
It covers both: how Doppler is actually extracted from LEO downlink signals, and how those Doppler measurements are used in EKF to estimate position.

It is **not** satellite to satellite relative OD and also assumes the satellite state is known. It is methodologically relevant, but not same problem we are trying to solve.
