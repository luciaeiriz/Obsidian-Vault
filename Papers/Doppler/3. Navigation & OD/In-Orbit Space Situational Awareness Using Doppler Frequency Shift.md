### Persistent Notes
It addresses almost exactly the conceptual question: **if Doppler only gives us relative line-of-sight velocity, how can a sequence of Doppler measurements tell us anything about relative orbital geometry?**

The paper proposes that one spacecraft receives an active narrowband beacon from another spacecraft and uses **only the Doppler-frequency history** to estimate relative orbital parameters and ultimately the **closest-approach/miss distance**.

They are **not doing general six-state relative OD**. Instead the simplify by describing the relative geometry using only the relative inclination between orbital planes and the relative orbital phase/true-anomaly offset.
