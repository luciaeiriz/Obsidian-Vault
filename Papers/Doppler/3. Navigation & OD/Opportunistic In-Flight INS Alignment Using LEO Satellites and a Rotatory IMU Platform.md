### Persistent Notes
This paper does use **LEO Doppler measurements in a navigation filter**, but its main purpose is **INS alignment and calibration**, not orbit determination. The authors use Doppler-derived pseudorange-rate measurements from LEO satellites to estimate the state of a moving receiver, then use that solution to help calibrate an inertial navigation system. 

They convert measured Doppler to pseudorange
