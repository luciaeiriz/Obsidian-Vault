
##### 1. What do VHF and S-band actually mean?

A satellite communicates by transmitting an electromagnetic wave at some carrier frequency $f_c$.

- **VHF** means _Very High Frequency_. Broadly, VHF covers $30–300~\mathrm{MH}z$. 
- **S-band** is a microwave-frequency band, broadly around $2–4~\mathrm{GHz}.$ 

Therefore the S-band carrier oscillates faster than the VHF carrier.

##### 2. Why does carrier frequency matter for Doppler?

Since

$\lambda=\frac{c}{f_c},$

VHF wavelength is larger than S-band wavelength. 

Remember our Doppler equation:

$D=-\frac{f_c}{c}\dot\rho$

Equivalently,

$D=-\frac{\dot\rho}{\lambda}.$

For the **same physical satellite motion**, the shorter S-band wavelength means that the motion passes through many more carrier cycles per second.

Therefore the frequency shift in Hz is larger.
